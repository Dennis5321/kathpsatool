📊 WHO Body Mass Index (BMI) Reference

Body Mass Index (BMI) is an international screening indicator calculated from your height and weight:

🔹 Underweight < 18.5 kg/m²
✅ Normal weight 18.5 – 24.9 kg/m²
⚠️ Overweight 25.0 – 29.9 kg/m²
🚨 Obese 30.0+ kg/m²
📌 Formula: Body Mass Index (BMI) = Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]2
🏥 KATH Pre-Consultation Tip

Having your accurate Body Mass Index (BMI) ready prior to seeing a physician helps clinicians speed up your vital checks and recommend the right nutrition and dosage plans.
