Komfo Anokye Teaching Hospital (KATH)

One of Ghana's premier public referral hospitals, proudly serving the Ashanti Region and beyond with world-class healthcare since 1954.

📜

Our History

Established in 1954 in Kumasi, KATH has grown from a regional facility into Ghana's second-largest hospital, with over 1,000 beds and serving millions of patients yearly. It is named after the legendary Ashanti king and military strategist, Komfo Anokye.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide excellent, equitable, and compassionate health services; promote research, training, and education; and partner with communities for improved health outcomes across Ghana and the sub-region.

🌟

Our Vision

To be a world-class teaching hospital that is a centre of excellence for health services, training, and research, recognized throughout Africa and beyond.

💎

Our Core Values

Integrity · Excellence · Compassion · Teamwork · Accountability · Respect. These values guide every interaction with patients, families, and colleagues.
