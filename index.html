Welcome to the KATH
Self-Assessment Tool

Answer a few quick questions about your health to get a Body Mass Index (BMI) reading and a suggested KATH directorate to visit — right before your consultation.

🚀 Start Assessment

⚡ How It Works

Three simple steps — takes less than 2 minutes

⚖️

Step 1 — Body Mass Index (BMI) Check

Enter your name, age, gender, height, and weight. We calculate your Body Mass Index instantly.

🔍

Step 2 — Symptoms

Select any symptoms you are experiencing. Multiple selections are allowed.

📄

Step 3 — Results

View your Body Mass Index (BMI) result and the recommended KATH directorate to visit.

⚠️ Important Notice

This tool is for educational and self-assessment purposes only and does not replace professional medical diagnosis. Please consult a KATH staff member for proper evaluation.
